Blood Donation Awareness

What is Blood Donation?

Blood donation is the process of voluntarily giving blood to help patients who need blood during surgeries, accidents, or medical treatments.

Who Can Donate Blood?

  • Healthy individuals between 18 and 60 years
  • Minimum weight should be 50 kg
  • No serious illness

Who Should Not Donate Blood?

  • People with infectious diseases
  • Pregnant women
  • People with low hemoglobin

Benefits of Blood Donation

  • Saves lives
  • Improves blood circulation
  • Encourages community support
